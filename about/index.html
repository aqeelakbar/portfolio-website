About

I design and build products that make complex decisions easier to understand and trust.

I’m Aqeel, a Senior UX Designer and UX Engineer who works from research and product definition through to frontend implementation.

01 · My path

Design decisions that survive delivery.

I started my career in frontend development and gradually moved deeper into UX research, interaction design and product strategy. That path means I can investigate a problem, shape the product response and stay involved as the experience moves into code.

Over more than 10 years, I’ve worked in an experience-design team at Amazon, on enterprise tools and design systems at Philips, on design-system work at The Adecco Group, and on environmental decision-support software at Sproutfull.

I’m most effective where users need to understand complex information, follow demanding workflows and make decisions with confidence. I’m also exploring how generative AI can support judgement without hiding uncertainty or taking control away from people.

02 · How I work

From the real decision to the working interface.

01

Understand the real decision

Research the people, rules, data and constraints behind the interface—not just the requested feature.

02

Make complexity inspectable

Structure workflows, evidence and uncertainty so people can understand what matters and decide what to do next.

03

Carry intent into implementation

Use prototypes and frontend code to test assumptions, expose technical constraints and preserve important decisions through delivery.

03 · Design and code

Close enough to implementation to test the decisions.

I work close enough to production to understand what an interface demands from the system behind it. I prototype and implement in React, TypeScript and Vue, collaborate with engineers on data and API constraints, and use design systems and accessibility testing to keep design and delivery aligned.

Product and interaction design

Figma, research, journey mapping, information architecture, prototyping and usability testing.

Interface implementation

React, TypeScript, Vue and Next.js for prototypes and production-facing interfaces.

Systems and quality

Storybook, design tokens, accessibility, axe-core and BrowserStack.

AI-assisted workflows

Exploring directions, testing assumptions and moving between design and implementation without hiding uncertainty.

04 · Beyond the work

Dog dad, plant dad and regular cyclist.

Outside work, I spend a lot of time with my dog, looking after my plants and cycling whenever I can. I enjoy understanding how things work, but I’m just as interested in the small ways people adapt when something does not work for them.
